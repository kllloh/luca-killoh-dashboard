Scouting Report

Where Luca's at, and what to work on next

Luca has been the standout player across both divisions this season. He went 3–0 and unbeaten in U10 Boys Division 4, leading the whole division in scoring at 13.3 PPG, before Hawks KO were promoted into a tougher Division 3. Since the move, he's continued to lead the competition in scoring at 11.0 PPG — though it's now a tight race with Hudson Bromley (10.89). The Hawks have also picked up a win and a draw in their two most recent games, climbing from last place to 5th on the Division 3 ladder.

Strengths
  • Elite, consistent scoring — still #1 in Division 3 PPG (11.0), just ahead of Hudson Bromley (10.89)
  • Sharing the ball more — his share of the team's points has eased from 73% to 67% as teammates like Ted Bramich (34 points) step up
  • High-volume finishing — 69 scoring plays this season, 59 of them clean 2-pointers, plus 10 free throws and 0 fouls in his first 3 games
Focus areas
  • Foul discipline — 0 fouls across Division 4, but 19 in 8 Division 3 games (~2.4/game); tightening up positioning would keep him on the floor in close games
  • Finishing close games — three of the last five Division 3 results have been decided by 3 points or less (a 1-point loss, a 3-point loss, and a draw); composure in the last few minutes could flip these into wins
  • Bouncing back when contained — Round 5 (7 points in a 26-point loss) and Round 12 (10 points in a draw) show what physical defenses can do; keep finding ways to score when the first look isn't there
