Analysis — Winter Scouting Report

Luca was the standout player across both divisions in Winter 2026. He went 3–0 and unbeaten in Division 4, leading the whole division in scoring at 13.3 PPG, before Hawks KO were promoted into a tougher Division 3. Since the move, he finished as the competition’s top scorer at 11.2 PPG, just ahead of Hudson Bromley (11.0). Hawks KO closed the regular season with a win and two draws in their last three games to finish 5th on the Division 3 ladder, just outside the top-4 finals places.

Strengths

  • Elite, consistent scoring — finished the Division 3 regular season #1 in PPG (11.2), narrowly ahead of Hudson Bromley (11.0)
  • Sharing the ball more — his share of the team’s points held around 67% (down from 73% earlier) as teammates like Ted Bramich (41 points) kept contributing
  • High-volume finishing — 77 scoring plays in Winter, 64 of them clean 2-pointers, plus 13 free throws and 0 fouls in his first 3 games

Focus areas

  • Foul discipline — 0 fouls across Division 4, but 20 in 9 Division 3 games (~2.2/game); tighter positioning would keep him on the floor in close games
  • Finishing close games — three of the last five Division 3 results were decided by 3 points or less (a 3-point loss and back-to-back draws); composure in the final minutes could turn these into wins
  • Bouncing back when contained — Round 5 (7 points in a 26-point loss) and Round 12 (10 points in a draw) show what physical defenses can do; keep finding ways to score when the first look isn’t there
